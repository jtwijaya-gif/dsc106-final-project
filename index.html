Project Progress

What have we done so far?

So far, our team has decided to focus on how different emissions scenarios could affect global temperatures by 2100. We chose the CMIP6 climate dataset and created five static visualizations to explore temperature patterns across different years and scenarios. We also planned out how our interactive website will work, including a global temperature map, a line chart, and controls for selecting different scenarios and years. We have started building the basic structure of our website and set up our GitHub repository so everyone can contribute. Our next step is to turn our static visualizations into interactive ones that people can explore themselves.

What will be the most challenging part of your project to design, and why?

We think the most challenging part will be making the global temperature map interactive while keeping it easy to understand. Since our dataset covers many locations, years, and emissions scenarios, we need to figure out how to display all that information without making the visualization too complicated. We also want the year slider and scenario selection to update the map smoothly so users can compare different outcomes. Another challenge will be connecting the map with the line chart while making sure the colors and labels clearly show temperature changes. Our goal is to make the website interactive and informative without overwhelming the reader.
